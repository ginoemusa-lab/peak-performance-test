Nutrition Guide
Calorie Calculator

You do not need to count every calorie. You do not need a meal plan. You need three rules and one number. Follow these for 21 days and your nutrition will take care of itself.

The Three Rules
1
Anchor with protein
Build every meal around a protein source first. Chicken, beef, fish, eggs, Greek yoghurt, cottage cheese. Everything else fills in around it. Hit your protein target and the rest largely takes care of itself.
2
Colour the plate
Add vegetables to at least two meals per day. Spinach, broccoli, peppers, cucumber, tomatoes. No specific amounts — just add colour. Fibre, vitamins, and volume for almost zero calories.
3
Limit ultra-processed food
If it comes in a wrapper with more than five ingredients — limit it. Not eliminate. Limit. One rule: if you eat something processed, pair it with a protein source. It automatically reduces the damage.
Your Protein Target
Protein protects your muscle while you are in a calorie deficit. Without enough protein your body breaks down muscle for energy — you lose weight but not fat. Use the Calorie Calculator tab to get your exact personalised target.

The 3 Daily Habits
👟
8,000 → 10,000 → 12,000 steps daily (increases each week)
Walking burns significant calories without spiking hunger hormones the way intense cardio does. Step goal increases each week to prevent a plateau — 8k in week 1, 10k in week 2, 12k in week 3. Screenshot your count in the evening check-in.
🥩
Hit your protein target every day
Your most important number. Not calories, not carbs — protein. Log it in MyFitnessPal or Cronometer. Screenshot your daily total in the group check-in. If you hit nothing else — hit this.
💧
3 litres of water every day — done by 8pm
Dehydration disguises itself as hunger. Most men eating excess calories are simply under-hydrated. Use a marked 1L bottle — fill it three times. Done by 8pm so sleep is not disrupted.
5 High Protein Meals That Fit Your Life
Meal 01
Greek Yoghurt Breakfast Bowl
500g Greek yoghurt, mixed berries, handful of granola, drizzle of honey. Quick to prepare, high protein, keeps hunger low until lunch.
Protein 35g
Cals ~420
Meal 02
Chicken Rice and Broccoli
200g grilled chicken breast, 150g cooked rice, steamed broccoli, hot sauce or soy sauce. The most reliable high protein meal in existence.
Protein 52g
Cals ~520
Meal 03
Scrambled Eggs on Toast
4 whole eggs scrambled, 2 slices sourdough or wholegrain toast, spinach on the side. Fast, cheap, high protein. Add smoked salmon to push protein higher.
Protein 34g
Cals ~480
Meal 04
Lean Beef Mince Bowl
200g lean beef mince, rice or sweet potato, peppers and onion, seasoned with cumin and paprika. Batch cook Sunday — eat across the week.
Protein 48g
Cals ~550
Meal 05
Protein Shake and Banana
2 scoops whey protein in water or skimmed milk, one banana. Not a meal replacement — a protein gap filler. Use when short on time or struggling to hit your protein target by evening.
Protein 45–55g
Cals ~380
What to Limit
Limit these
  • Alcohol — empty calories, disrupts sleep and fat loss
  • Sugary drinks — juice, fizzy drinks, energy drinks
  • Fast food more than once per week
  • Ultra-processed snacks — crisps, chocolate, biscuits
  • Excessive sauces — ketchup, mayo, creamy dressings
Not banned — just be aware
  • White rice and white bread are fine — portion matters
  • Fruit is fine — do not avoid it for sugar content
  • Coffee is fine — avoid adding sugar
  • One social meal per week will not break your progress
  • Imperfect days happen — they do not restart the clock
